Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

PathPilot is an AI-assisted planning and guidance application. This policy explains the information PathPilot may process, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information we process

  • Account information such as name and email address.
  • Authentication and security records needed to keep accounts signed in and protected. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not plaintext.
  • Content users submit to PathPilot, including prompts, preferences, feedback, and conversations needed to provide the requested features.
  • Limited operational information such as feature usage, errors, and security or account events used to maintain and improve the service.

How information is used

Information is used to provide PathPilot features, create and secure accounts, send transactional messages such as email verification or password-reset links, improve product quality, investigate errors and abuse, and maintain service reliability.

AI and external services

PathPilot may send relevant request content to third-party services that power specific features, such as AI generation, web research, hosting, or transactional email delivery. Only information needed for the requested feature should be sent. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Google and Gmail API use

PathPilot uses the Gmail API only for the application's own transactional email account to send messages such as verification and password-reset emails. PathPilot does not use this integration to read, download, or analyze end users' Gmail inboxes.

Data retention and security

PathPilot uses reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect stored information. Data may be retained while an account is active and for a limited period afterward when needed for security, troubleshooting, legal obligations, or service integrity. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

User choices

Users may choose not to provide optional information. Account-related requests, privacy questions, or deletion requests can be sent to the contact address below. Some records may need to be retained where required for security or legal reasons.

Children

PathPilot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PathPilot develops. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
